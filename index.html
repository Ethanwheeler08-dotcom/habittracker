How scoring works

  • +1 point for each habit you tick today. Untick it and the point comes back off.
  • Perfect day when all 5 are ticked. String 7 together for a +5 bonus, then the counter starts a new week.
  • Miss a day and the perfect-day streak drops back to 0. Missing a day never takes points away.
  • Forgot to tick? Use the Yesterday tab to fill in the day before. It counts as if you ticked on time. Only yesterday can be changed, and only until midnight.
  • Went over 1,800 cals: −5 points (never below 0) and today's calorie box is unticked.
  • Ate fast food: Current Points and the streak reset to 0, and the date goes into Wipeout History.
  • XP and levels count every point you've ever earned, so penalties never take them away.
  • Rewards unlock from your Personal Best, so a wipeout never locks a reward again.
